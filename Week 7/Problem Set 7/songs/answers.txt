Audio Aura Overview:
An “audio aura” can be thought of as a listener’s distinctive musical personality, shaped by the characteristics of the songs they enjoy. Using the top 100 tracks from the songs.db database, this aura can be described by examining several audio attributes, including danceability, energy, valence, tempo, and speechiness.
Danceability:
When the average danceability value is high, it suggests the listener gravitates toward music with rhythms that are easy to move to—often genres like pop, electronic, or dance.
Energy:
Elevated energy scores imply a preference for more dynamic, high-intensity tracks, which may include genres such as rock or hip-hop.
Valence:
A higher valence average indicates a tendency toward upbeat, positive-sounding songs, while lower valence values hint at a liking for more emotional or reflective music.
Tempo:
Faster average tempos point to an interest in quick, lively songs; slower tempos suggest the listener may enjoy more laid-back or mellow tracks.
Speechiness:
High speechiness values reveal that the user may favor music with more spoken content, such as rap or spoken-word styles.
Limitations
Relying only on a single set of 100 songs from one year provides an incomplete picture for several reasons:
Changing Preferences:
Musical tastes shift over time, so selections from 2018 might not reflect current interests.
Context and Mood:
The user’s emotional state or life circumstances during that year may have influenced what they listened to at the time.
Small Dataset:
A list of just 100 tracks isn’t enough to capture the full scope of someone’s musical preferences.

Possible Improvements:
Broader Time Window:
Examining songs the listener chose over multiple years would provide a more stable and accurate representation of their evolving taste.
Playlist Examination:
Looking at various user-created playlists can reveal the range of genres, moods, and contexts they enjoy.
Use of Additional Metadata:
Incorporating details such as genres, subgenres, or artist relationships would add more nuance to the audio aura analysis.
Direct User Input:
Using feedback like song ratings or “liked” tracks can significantly refine the profile.

Combining these strategies would lead to a deeper, more reliable understanding of a listener’s audio aura.
